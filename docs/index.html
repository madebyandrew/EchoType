Privacy

Private by architecture,
not by promise.

Every popular dictation app today ships your voice to someone else's servers. Some also take screenshots of your screen for “context.” You're asked to trust a privacy policy — a document that can change — instead of an architecture that can't.

EchoType takes the opposite bet: the most private data pipeline is the one that doesn't exist. Your voice is transcribed on-device by Whisper, Metal-accelerated on your own GPU. There is no cloud. There is no account. The app makes zero network calls — unplug your Wi-Fi and it works exactly the same, because the internet was never part of the pipeline. The temporary audio file is deleted the moment transcription finishes.

Features

Small app. Sharp edges filed off.

hover a card

Works everywhere your cursor does.

Any text field in any app — native, Electron, browser, terminal. If you can type there, you can speak there.

Any key you want.

Right Option is the default, but assign anything: F-keys, Fn, Right Command. Press-and-hold, or use Toggle Mode.

Fast by default, accurate on demand.

Keeps up with conversation. Want more accuracy or another language? Point the config at a bigger Whisper model.

Cleans up after you.

Optional filler-word removal strips the ums and uhs, so the page reads like you meant it.

Long dictations, instantly.

Paste mode inserts everything at once via ⌘V — and politely restores your clipboard after.

Stays out of the way.

It's a menu bar icon. Recording shows 🔴, transcribing shows ✍️ — that's the whole interface.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and MIT-licensed. No trial, no pro tier, no catch.

What are the requirements?

Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 13+.

What permissions does it need?

Two: Accessibility (to hear your key and type for you) and Microphone. Both explained in plain language on first run, both revocable anytime in System Settings.

Does any audio ever leave my Mac?

No. Zero network calls. Transcription runs on your GPU via Whisper; the temporary audio file is deleted the moment transcription finishes.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Not yet — EchoType is built natively for Apple Silicon. Star the GitHub repo to follow progress.

Can I use other languages or bigger models?

Yes — point the config at any Whisper model. One download, one line.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madebyandrew/EchoType/main/install.sh | bash

Apple Silicon · macOS 13+ · ~130 MB · MIT licensed · everything runs on your Mac

Or download the app manually

Download EchoType.zip, unzip it, and drag EchoType.app to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS blocks it because EchoType isn't notarized yet: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. One time only. The command above skips this entirely.
